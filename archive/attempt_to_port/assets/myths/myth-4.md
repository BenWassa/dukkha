# Myth 4: "I can get addicted to dopamine itself — I need a 'dopamine detox' to reset my brain."

**Truth:** You build tolerance to overstimulation, not addiction to the chemical itself. High-salience inputs flood receptors, lowering sensitivity; reducing overstimulation lets baseline sensitivity return.

**One Action:** Choose one day to avoid high-salience inputs. Lower the threshold so simple things become pleasurable again.

**Sources:** Volkow et al. (2016) — tolerance and receptor down-regulation.
