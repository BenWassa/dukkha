# Myth 2: "Dopamine is the 'pleasure chemical' – more dopamine means more happiness."

**Truth:** Dopamine drives wanting (incentive salience), not necessarily liking or hedonic pleasure. Sky-high wanting can coexist with flat enjoyment, which is why compulsive checking persists even when it no longer feels good.

**One Action:** Ask “Am I wanting or liking?” before a habit—prioritize what you truly enjoy over what merely itches.

**Sources:** Berridge & Robinson (2016, 1998); Wyvell & Berridge (2000); Evans et al. (2006).
