# Myth 5: "Buddhism teaches that we should eliminate all pleasure and become emotionless monks."

**Truth:** The goal is equanimity and balance, not suppression. The Middle Way rejects both indulgence and harsh self-denial; true equanimity is feeling emotions fully without being tossed around by them.

**One Action:** Practice Mindful Appreciation: enjoy pleasant moments without gripping them and meet unpleasant moments without fearing they will last forever.

**Sources:** Gethin, R. (1998) — the Middle Way teaching.
