# Myth 3: "All desire is bad; I should become emotionless and want nothing."

**Truth:** Craving (taṇhā) causes suffering; aspiration (chanda) fuels progress. Buddhist teaching targets the compulsive, sticky thirst that leads to dukkha—not wholesome intentions to act, create, or help.

**One Action:** Label your drive: is it “must have” (contracted, sticky) or “choose to” (expansive, clear)? Feed aspiration, not craving.

**Sources:** Bodhi, B. (2000) — definitions of taṇhā; Harvey, P. (2012) — wholesome vs. unwholesome desire.
