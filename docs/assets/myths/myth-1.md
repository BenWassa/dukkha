# Myth 1: "Buddhism says 'Life is Suffering,' so it's a pessimistic philosophy."

**Truth:** Dukkha signifies unsatisfactoriness, not just pain. Even pleasant experiences are impermanent; clinging to them fuels frustration and craving. The First Noble Truth names this pervasive incompleteness so a path to its cessation can be followed.

**One Action:** Practice Mindful Impermanence — fully appreciate pleasant moments while acknowledging their temporary nature to lessen clinging.

**Sources:** Rahula, W. (1974) — *What the Buddha Taught*; Bodhi, B. (2000) — *The Connected Discourses*.
